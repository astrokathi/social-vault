Empty States & iOS Share Intake Architecture

Instructional Zero-Data Design, Apple's App Groups Limitation, and the Clipboard Detection Pattern.

1. The Problem with Instagram Sharing on iOS

When you tap the Share button on an Instagram Reel, Instagram displays its internal direct message (DM) interface, not the iOS system share sheet.

Furthermore, even if you tap "Share to..." in Instagram, iOS only displays third-party apps in the system share sheet if they include an iOS Share Extension (.appex) communicating via an App Group.

2. The Industry-Standard Clipboard Pattern

Every prominent Instagram utility on iOS (such as Repost and InSaver) relies on the Clipboard Intake Pattern:

  1. User taps Share ➔ Copy Link inside Instagram (1 single tap).
  2. User switches to ReelVault.
  3. ReelVault monitors WidgetsBindingObserver.didChangeAppLifecycleState().
  4. When state transitions to AppLifecycleState.resumed, the app inspects Clipboard.getData().
  5. If an Instagram URL is detected, ReelVault displays an instant "Save to Vault" action banner.

3. Instructional Empty State Banners

When a user clears sample data, a blank black screen feels broken. ReelVault solves this with an Instructional Empty State featuring:

  • A visual 3-step guide explaining how to copy from Instagram.
  • A primary "Paste Instagram Link" button reading the clipboard or prompting manual entry.
  • A sandbox "Test with Demo Reel" button to verify video extraction anytime.
